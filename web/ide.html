Instance

A game maker that runs in your browser: a clean-room reimplementation of GameMaker 8.1, with its editors, its GML scripting and its runner. Open an example and change it, or just play. Best on a desktop computer: the editor is made for a mouse, and the games need a keyboard.

Brickhop Start here

A platformer about carrying, stacking and throwing bricks: six levels in two worlds. Arrows walk, Z jumps, X picks up and throws a brick.

Edit opens its first level in the room editor: move a platform, add bricks or enemies, then press Run (F5) to play your version. The other levels are under Rooms in the tree.

Spelunky Classic

Derek Yu's 2008 GameMaker game: a cave-diving platformer with randomly generated levels. Arrows move, Z jumps, X whips or uses, Up goes through doors.
