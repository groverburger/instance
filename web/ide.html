Instance

A game maker that runs in your browser: a clean-room reimplementation of GameMaker 8.1, with its editors, its GML scripting and its runner. Pick an example and change it, or just play. Best on a desktop computer: the editor is made for a mouse, and the games need a keyboard.

Brickhop Start here

A platformer about carrying, stacking and throwing bricks. Edit opens its first level in the room editor: move a platform, add bricks or enemies, then press Run (F5) to play your version.

Arrows walk, Z jumps, X picks up and throws a brick.

Gravity Grade

A falling-block game after the arcade "grand master" games: levels 0 to 999, gravity that climbs to 20G, and grades. All of it is GML, under Scripts and Objects.

Arrows move and drop, Z and X rotate.

Spelunky Classic

Derek Yu's 2008 GameMaker game: a cave-diving platformer with randomly generated levels.

Arrows move, Z jumps, X whips or uses, Up goes through doors.
